# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-13_

**This app collects nothing about you for itself.** It shows ClickUp's own website.

- **Internet:** the tabs load ClickUp (`clickup.com` and the services ClickUp's website uses),
  exactly like a browser would, and while you log in, the sign-in page you choose (Google,
  Microsoft, Apple or your company's). What you do there is covered by
  [ClickUp's privacy policy](https://clickup.com/privacy). Other links open in your own browser.
- The app itself sends nothing anywhere: no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and
  no update checks (automatic updates are off).
- **On your PC** (`%APPDATA%\Better ClickUp`):
  - `session.json`: the addresses and titles of your open tabs, the window's size and position,
    and the zoom level, so they come back after a restart.
  - `Partitions\clickup\`: what a browser keeps for ClickUp: your login (encrypted with your
    Windows account), cache and site storage.
- **Notifications** are shown by Windows and appear in its notification centre.
- **To remove everything:** uninstall the app, then delete `%APPDATA%\Better ClickUp`.

If this changes, this file will be updated **before** the change is released, describing what is
collected or sent, why, where it is stored, and how to delete it.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
