# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-14_

**This app collects nothing.**

- It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
- It does not connect to the internet: no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no
  update checks (automatic updates are off).
- Anything the app may keep in the future stays on your PC, in your Windows user profile
  (`%APPDATA%\<App name>`).

If this changes, this file will be updated **before** the change is released, describing what is
collected or sent, why, where it is stored, and how to delete it.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
